# CREDO-TRADAI Risk Rules

CREDO-TRADAI is designed to research and paper-trade before any real-money execution.

## Default safeguards
- Default risk per trade: 1%
- Maximum configured risk per trade: 2% for the dashboard's recommended mode
- Never risk money needed for living, school, or essential expenses.
- A stop-loss should be defined before a paper trade is opened.
- Avoid opening unlimited simultaneous positions.
- Backtests must be reviewed for fees, spreads, slippage and changing market conditions before being treated as evidence.

## Important
Signals are analytical outputs, not guarantees. Backtest results are historical simulations and do not predict future returns.
